# Scalefort Outsourcing - Website Project Brief

## Overview

Build a **single-page informational React website** for **Scalefort Outsourcing**, a company that helps businesses outsource skilled tech talent across the full technology delivery lifecycle.

Scalefort is not positioned around one narrow specialty. The company helps clients access flexible talent for engineering, product, design, cloud, data, QA, technical support and technology operations.

The site's core goal is to convince potential clients to reach out and start a conversation. It is a **marketing/lead generation** page, not a dashboard or app.

## Services Offered

### 1. Engineering Talent
- **Headline:** Software Engineering Teams
- **Value prop:** Extend your team with developers who can build, maintain and modernize reliable digital products.
- **Example roles:**
  - Frontend developers
  - Backend developers
  - Full-stack developers
  - Mobile developers
  - API and integration engineers
  - QA engineers
- **Key benefits:**
  - Frontend & Backend Engineers - Skilled developers across modern web, mobile, API and platform technologies
  - Full-Stack Delivery - Talent that can move across product layers and ship complete features
  - Quality Engineering - Clean code, testing discipline and delivery practices that reduce risk
  - Scalable Capacity - Add engineering capacity quickly as product demand grows
- **Quote bubble:** "Great Code. Faster Delivery. Real Results."

### 2. Product & Design Talent
- **Headline:** Product, UI/UX & Digital Teams
- **Value prop:** Work with product-minded talent who can turn ideas, requirements and customer needs into usable digital experiences.
- **Example roles:**
  - UI/UX designers
  - Product designers
  - Product coordinators
  - Business analysts
  - UX researchers
  - Design system contributors
- **Key benefits:**
  - UI/UX Designers - Designers who create clear, polished and user-friendly interfaces
  - Product Support - Product coordinators and analysts who keep priorities, scope and delivery aligned
  - User-Centered Thinking - Talent that understands customers, journeys and business outcomes
  - Launch-Ready Execution - Support for prototypes, MVPs, redesigns and product improvements
- **Quote bubble:** "Better Products. Clearer Experiences."

### 3. Cloud, Data & Operations Talent
- **Headline:** Cloud, Data, Support & Tech Ops
- **Value prop:** Keep your technology running with cloud, data, QA, support and operations talent that strengthens daily delivery.
- **Example roles:**
  - Cloud engineers
  - DevOps engineers
  - Data analysts
  - QA testers
  - Technical support agents
  - IT and technology operations specialists
- **Key benefits:**
  - Cloud & DevOps Support - Talent for infrastructure, deployment, automation and reliability workflows
  - Data & Analytics Talent - Analysts and data specialists who help teams understand and use their information
  - Technical Support Teams - Responsive support talent for customers, internal users and product operations
  - Reliable Coverage - Flexible support across time zones, workloads and changing business needs
- **Quote bubble:** "Stronger Systems. Smoother Operations."

## Target Audience

- Startups and SMEs looking to scale without hiring overhead

- Businesses digitizing operations or modernizing existing systems
- Companies that need product, design, engineering, support, cloud, data or QA talent
- Decision-makers: CTOs, COOs, Founders, Product leads, Operations managers
